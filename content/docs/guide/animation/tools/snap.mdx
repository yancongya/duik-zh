# ![](/docs-img/duik/icons/snap.svg) Snap keyframes

![RXLAB_VIDEO](https://rxlaboratory.org/wp-content/uploads/rx-videos/Duik17_J03_AnimTools02__EN_720.mp4)  
*This video is part of [__the official comprehensive video course about Duik Ángela__](https://rxlaboratory.org/product/the-official-comprehensive-video-course-about-duik-angela/)*

Very often, when you've retimed an animation, some keyframes may end up between actual frames of the composition. You can fix this by **selecting all your keyframes** and click on the ![](/docs-img/duik/icons/snap.svg) ***Snap*** button; this will move all the out-of-place keyframes to actual frames.
