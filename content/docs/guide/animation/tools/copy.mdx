# ![](/docs-img/duik/icons/copy.svg) ![](/docs-img/duik/icons/paste.svg) Copy, cut and paste animation

<Video src="https://rxlaboratory.org/wp-content/uploads/rx-videos/Duik17_J01_AnimTools01__EN_720.mp4" caption={"This video is part of [__the official comprehensive video course about Duik Ángela__](https://rxlaboratory.org/product/the-official-comprehensive-video-course-about-duik-angela/)"} />

You can copy or cut and paste animations from multiple layers at once, and from one composition to another.

- **Select** some keyframes
- Click the ![](/docs-img/duik/icons/copy.svg) ***Copy animation*** button.  
    - `[Alt] + [Click]` to **cut** the keyframes instead of copying.
- **Select the destination** layers, and **move the time indicator** to the time when you wish to paste the animation.
- Click the ![](/docs-img/duik/icons/paste.svg) ***Paste animation*** button.  
    - `[Alt] + [Click]` to reverse the animation.
    - `[Ctrl] + [Click]` to offset the animation from the current values (instead of replacing the current values).

A few other options are available when pasting the animation, `[Shift] + [Click]` the ![](/docs-img/duik/icons/paste.svg) *Paste animation* button to access them.
