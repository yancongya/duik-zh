FAQ

You've got questions. We've got answers

Short answers to the most-asked. For everything else, write to hello@linearity.io

How is Linearity different from AI image generators?

Linearity creates editable design files, not flat images.

Is Linearity a Canva alternative?

Yes, but built around AI. Canva gives you editable templates you adjust by hand. Linearity's AI design generator creates the whole campaign for you from a prompt, then keeps it on-brand and handles more complex, layered designs Canva's templates can't. See our Canva alternative page for a side-by-side.

Are outputs fully editable?

Yes, all generated vectors, text layers, and paths are fully editable inside the Linearity editor.

Do I need to be a designer?

No! Linearity's AI features are designed to help anyone create professional designs regardless of their experience.

Can I use my brand assets?

Yes, you can import and apply your custom logos, color palettes, and custom fonts.

Can Linearity resize designs for different channels?

Yes, with our auto-resize feature, you can adapt your designs for any social media platform in seconds.

Is Linearity built for teams?

Yes, we support collaborative workspaces, shared design assets, and team roles/permissions.

What happens to Curve?

Vectornator has rebranded to Linearity Curve, bringing all vector tools under the new Linearity brand.

Do you train on my designs?

No, we respect your privacy. We do not use your private designs to train our machine learning models.

Can I use outputs commercially?

Yes, you have full ownership and commercial rights to everything you export from Linearity.

What file formats can I export?

You can export in PNG, JPEG, SVG, PDF, and Linearity document formats.
